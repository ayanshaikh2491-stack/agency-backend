# Qualification Workflow

## Overview
- **Purpose**: Qualify leads identified by the Prospecting Workflow.
- **Agents**: SBA
- **Trigger**: New lead created
- **Checkpoint**: SBA review (no external contact)
- **Push Right**: Internal analysis only
- **Brief**: Qualification report with score and notes

## Tasks
1. **Analyze Lead Data**:
   - **Agent**: SBA
   - **Task**: Review lead data and assign a qualification score.
   - **Output**: Lead data with qualification score.

2. **Assign Score**:
   - **Agent**: SBA
   - **Task**: Assign a score to the lead based on analysis.
   - **Output**: Lead with assigned score.

3. **Add Notes**:
   - **Agent**: SBA
   - **Task**: Add notes to the lead based on analysis.
   - **Output**: Lead with notes.

## Output
- **Qualification Report**: Lead data with qualification score and notes.
