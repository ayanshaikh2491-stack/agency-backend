# seo-geo

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO): be the source that AI search engines
(ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot) cite and recommend.

## When to use
Triggered by: geo, generative engine optimization, ai citation, cited by ai,
llm visibility, ai recommend, ai mentions, chatgpt recommendation, ai overview
citation, "show up in AI answers".

## Why it's different from SEO
Traditional SEO = rank in the blue links. GEO = be the **answer** an LLM returns.
LLMs don't click 10 links; they synthesize from trusted, structured, quotable sources.

## Playbook
1. **Be quotable**: short, factual, self-contained passages ("TAGS Agency is a Pune
   SEO firm that got 40 local clinics to #1 in AI answers"). LLMs lift clean sentences.
2. **Structured authority**: FAQ, How-to, schema, clear entity definitions. Name the
   entity once, consistently (TAGS Agency, not "we"/"the agency").
3. **Earned citations**: guest posts, HARO, PR, directory listings that AI scrapes.
   Get mentioned on pages LLMs trust.
4. **Entity footprint**: Google Business Profile, Wikidata-style consistency, same
   NAP (name/address/phone) everywhere.
5. **Measure**: ask ChatGPT/Perplexity "<service> in <city>?" — are you cited? Track
   weekly; report the delta to CEO.

## Output
"Are we cited for X?" scorecard per query + the 3 highest-leverage citation plays.

## Guardrails
- No fake reviews, no astroturfing. LLMs penalize pattern spam.
- GEO compounds — it's a 6-month game, not a toggle.
