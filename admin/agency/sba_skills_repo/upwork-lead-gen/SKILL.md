# upwork-lead-gen

Find and win freelance leads on Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer, and similar platforms.

## When to use
Triggered by: upwork, fiverr, freelancer, proposal, bid, job post, RFP, "find clients",
outsource, remote gig, contract work.

## Workflow (the agent runs this)
1. **Search** open jobs matching the agency's services (SEO, AI visibility, websites,
   social, lead-gen). Filter by: fixed-price or hourly, client history (>=1 hire,
   payment verified), budget floor (skip <$50 junk).
2. **Qualify** the client: read the brief, check "Client spent", "Hire rate", reviews.
   Skip "pay after delivery" / generic "I need a website" with no detail.
3. **Write the proposal** (no templates):
   - Open with proof: "I ranked a local clinic for 12 keywords in 6 weeks" not "I am
     a hard worker".
   - Restate THEIR goal in 1 line, then your approach in 3 bullets.
   - Ask 1 sharp question to show you read it.
   - CTA: "Can we hop on a 10-min call to nail the scope?"
4. **Follow-up** after 2-3 days if no reply: 1 line, add value (a free observation).

## Voice
Hinglish with the client when they write Hindi; English when formal. Never copy-paste
the job description back. Lead with outcome + proof.
