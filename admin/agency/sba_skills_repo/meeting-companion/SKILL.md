# Meeting Companion Skill
## Scheduling → Pre-Meeting → Live Meeting → Post-Meeting → Handoff

---

## Core Philosophy
"Meeting ek opportunity hai close karne ki. Har meeting ka ek purpose hona chahiye, ek agenda, aur ek next step. SBA ka kaam hai ki meeting se maximum value nikale."

Yeh skill SBA ko guide karti hai poore meeting lifecycle mein — scheduling se lekar handoff tak.

---

## Phase 1: Meeting Scheduling

### Timing Rules
- **Cold lead** (score < 40): Pehle 2-3 touchpoints, phir meeting ka poocho
- **Warm lead** (score 40-70): Direct meeting propose karo
- **Hot lead** (score 70+): ASAP meeting, CEO available karo

### Meeting Types

| Type | Duration | Perfect For |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Discovery Call | 20-30 min | First baat, qualification |
| Demo Call | 30-45 min | Solution dikhana |
| Proposal Call | 45-60 min | Pricing + final close |
| Follow-up Call | 15-20 min | Objection handle, nudge |
| CEO Handoff Call | 30 min | CEO intro, final close |

### Scheduling Script (Hinglish)

**For warm leads:**
"Bahut acchi baat hui aapke saath. Lagta hai hum aapki madad kar sakte hain. Kal ya parson 30 minute ki call chalegi? Main CEO ko bhi le ke aaunga jo aapko poora solution dikhayenge."

**After objection handling:**
"Main samajh gaya aapki concern. Ek baar CEO se baat karlo — woh aapko exactly bata denge kaise kaam karta hai. 20 minute ki call — aapko kuch nahi karna, bas sunni hai. Chalegi?"

### Follow-up Sequence After No-Reply
- Day 1: Meeting link + agenda bhejo
- Day 2: "Sir/ Ma'am, kal ki call ke liye confirm?"
- Day 3: "Lagta hai busy hain. Reschedule karte hain?"
- Day 5: Break-up: "Koi issue hai to batao, warna main assume karunga ki abhi time nahi hai."

## Phase 2: Pre-Meeting Preparation

### Pre-Meeting Checklist
- [ ] Lead ka naam + business confirm
- [ ] Previous baat ka recap ready
- [ ] Lead score review
- [ ] 3 objectives for this meeting
- [ ] Objections anticipate karo
- [ ] Demo ready (agar zaroorat ho)
- [ ] CEO brief karo (agar present hoga)

### Agenda Template (Send Before Meeting)
```
Subject: Our Call on [Date] — Agenda

Hi [Lead Name],

Here's what we'll cover in our 30-minute call:

1. Your current situation & challenges (5 min)
2. How TAGS Agency can help (10 min)
3. Pricing & packages (5 min)
4. Next steps (5 min)
5. Q&A (5 min)

See you there!

— [SBA Name]
TAGS Agency
```

### SBA Pre-Meeting Brief for CEO
Jab CEO meeting mein aata hai to SBA yeh brief de:

```
LEAD BRIEF
━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Lead: [Name], [Business]
Score: 75 (CHAMP)
Stage: Demo Call
Pain: [2-3 lines]
Budget: [Range]
Decision Maker: Yes/No
Red Flags: None
Goal of this call: Close for proposal stage
```

## Phase 3: Live Meeting Companion

### Meeting Structure (30 Min)

| Time | Phase | What SBA Does |
|------|-------|---------------|
| 0-3 min | Opening | Greeting, intro, agenda set |
| 3-12 min | Discovery | Pain points explore |
| 12-22 min | Solution | Demo ya pitch |
| 22-27 min | Objections | Handle concerns |
| 27-30 min | Close | Next step confirm |

### Opening Script (2 min)
"Namaste [Name], welcome! Aaj ki call ka agenda yeh hai — pehle aap apni situation batao, phir main/CEO aapko TAGS ka solution dikhayenge, aur last mein next steps decide karenge. Ready? To aap batao — aapke liye is time sabse badi challenge kya hai?"

### During Meeting — SBA Tactics

**When lead goes silent:**
"Koi question hai? Main aapka confusion clear kar doon pehle."

**When lead raises objection:**
"Accha point hai. Aapne sahi kaha. Lekin ek baat batao — [handle objection]. Yeh clear hua?"

**When lead seems interested:**
"Achha lag raha hai aapko? To agli step pe chalte hain — [next step]."

**When lead is hesitant:**
"Koi reservation hai jo aap share karna chahenge? Main help karne ke liye hoon."

### Live Note-Taking
SBA should take notes in this format during meeting:

```
LEAD: [Name]
DATE: [Date]

PAIN POINTS:
→ [Point 1] (verbatim)
→ [Point 2] (verbatim)

BUDGET: [mentioned figure]
TIMELINE: [mentioned]

OBJECTIONS RAISED:
→ [Objection 1] → [How handled]
→ [Objection 2] → [How handled]

LEAD MOOD: [Positive/Neutral/Negative]
LEAD RESPONSE: [haan/nahi/maybe]

NEXT STEP AGREED: [What]
```

## Phase 4: Post-Meeting Follow-up

### Immediate (Within 2 Hours)
1. Thank you message bhejo
2. Meeting summary bhejo
3. Next step confirm karo

**Thank You Template:**
"Thank you for your time [Name]! Acchi baat hui. Jaise humne discuss kiya — [recap main points]. Agli call [proposed date/time] pe. Koi question ho to batao!"

### Lead Response Action Plan

| Lead Said | SBA Action |
|-----------|------------|
| "Haan, karte hain!" | → Handoff to CEO, create workspace |
| "Mujhe sochna hai" | → Follow-up in 3 days with case study |
| "Budget issue hai" | → Alternate package propose karo |
| "Kisi aur se baat karo" | → Ask for referral |
| "Nahi, thank you" | → Graceful exit, nurture pipeline |
| Ghost | → Follow-up sequence start |

### Meeting Done — Update Lead Score
- Interest level dikha → score adjust
- Budget clarity mili → score adjust
- Timeline clear hua → score adjust

## Phase 5: Handoff Trigger

### When to Handoff to CEO

**Immediate handoff** (score ≥ 80 or lead said "haan"):
1. CEO ko brief do
2. Lead ki contact info + context
3. Meeting notes + analysis
4. Lead response timestamp

**Suggestions mode** (score < 80):
1. Analysis bhejo CEO ko
2. Batao kya missing hai
3. Suggest karo lead se kya poochna chahiye

### Handoff Brief Format
```
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
HANDOFF BRIEF — [Lead Name]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

SCORE: 85/100 (CHAMP)
VERDICT: Ready ✅

PAIN: [2-3 lines]
BUDGET: [range]
TIMELINE: [timeline]

MEETING SUMMARY:
[2-3 lines]

CEO, ab is lead ka workspace banao. Ready to go!
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

## Objection Handling (Quick Reference)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Expensive hai" | "Compare mat karo cost se — compare karo ROI se. Agar hum [outcome] deliver karein to kitna value hoga?" |
| "Abhi time nahi" | "Kab sahi time hoga? Kyunki problem abhi hai, baad mein aur badh jayegi." |
| "Already koi hai" | "Achha, kya woh deliver kar rahe hain? Agar nahi to hum better kar sakte hain." |
| "Soch ke batata hoon" | "Kya sochna hai? Main clear kar doon jo confusion hai." |
| "Budget nahi hai" | "Budget flexibility hai? Hum installment mein bhi kar sakte hain." |
| "Decision nahi mera" | "Decision maker kaun hai? Unse milwa do, main baat kar leta hoon." |
