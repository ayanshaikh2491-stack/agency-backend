# Lead Qualification Skill
## CHAMP / BANT / MEDDIC — SBA ke liye

---

## Core Philosophy
"Har lead equal nahi hota. Qualification ka matlab hai time waste karna band karo aur sirf un leads pe focus karo jo close honge."

SBA ka sabse important kaam: **sahi lead identify karna** jahan CEO ka time lagana worthwhile hai.

---

## Framework 1: CHAMP (Challenges, Authority, Money, Prioritization)

### Why CHAMP over BANT?
BANT (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline) purana ho chuka hai. Modern sales mein CHAMP zyada effective hai because:
- **Challenges** pehle poocho — pain point milte hain sales entry ke liye
- **Authority** — decision maker confirm karo
- **Money** — budget samjho
- **Prioritization** — timeline + urgency

### CHAMP Questions for SBA

**Challenges (Pain Points):**
- "Batao aapki sabse badi problem kya hai is waqt?"
- "Aapne pehle kya try kiya is problem ko solve karne ke liye?"
- "Agar ye problem 3 mahine mein bhi rahi to kya hoga?"
- "Is problem ki wajah se aapka kitna loss ho raha hai monthly?"

**Authority (Decision Power):**
- "Decision aap khud karte hain ya koi aur bhi involved hai?"
- "Aapke alawa is decision mein aur kaun log hain?"
- "Agar hum aapko solution dikha dein, to aap final yes kar sakte hain?"
- "Koi senior jo sign-off dega?"

**Money (Budget):**
- "Aapne is type ke solution ke liye budget allocate kiya hai?"
- "Aapka expected investment range kya hai?"
- "Agar ROI dikha dein to budget flexibility hai?"
- "Current solution pe kitna kharch kar rahe ho?"

**Prioritization (Timeline):**
- "Yeh kitna urgent hai aapke liye 1-10 scale pe?"
- "Kab tak solution chahiye aapko?"
- "Aur kya projects chal rahe hain jo isko compete kar rahe hain?"
- "Agar aaj solution mil jaye to implementation kab start kar sakte ho?"

## Framework 2: BANT (Classic — Quick Version)

Jab SBA ko **quick qualify** karna ho (e.g., cold DM pe):

**B**udget — "Iske liye budget hai?" 
**A**uthority — "Decision aapka hi hai?"
**N**eed — "Problem clear hai?"
**T**imeline — "Kab tak chahiye?"

**BANT Scoring:**
- 4/4 yes → Hot lead
- 3/4 yes → Warm lead
- 1-2/4 yes → Needs nurturing

---

## Framework 3: MEDDIC (Enterprise)

Jab lead **enterprise/ high-ticket** ho (₹5L+ deals):

**M**etrics — Kya measure karte ho? (ROI, time saved, revenue)
**E**conomic Buyer — Actual paise wala kaun?
**D**ecision Criteria — Kis basis pe decide karoge?
**D**ecision Process — Step-by-step process kya hai?
**I**mplicate Pain — Agar solve nahi hua to kitna loss?
**C**hampion — Andar se kaun support kar raha hai?

## Lead Scoring Framework

### 0-100 Scoring Matrix

| Factor | Weight | Max Score |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Pain clarity | 25% | 25 |
| Budget available | 25% | 25 |
| Authority confirmed | 20% | 20 |
| Timeline (≤3 months) | 15% | 15 |
| Engagement level | 15% | 15 |

### Scoring Guide

**Pain clarity (0-25):**
- 0: "Sab theek hai, bas dekh rahe hain"
- 10: "Kuch issues hain but urgent nahi"
- 20: "Specific problem hai, solve karna chahte hain"
- 25: "Pain point clear hai, pareshan hain"

**Budget (0-25):**
- 0: "Budget nahi hai / free chahiye"
- 10: "Dekhenge, pehle proposal lao"
- 20: "Budget hai, range batayi"
- 25: "Budget allocate hai, ready to invest"

**Authority (0-20):**
- 0: "Bas inquiry hai, koi nahi janta"
- 10: "Kisi aur ko involve karna hoga"
- 15: "Decision maker hai but sign-off chahiye"
- 20: "Final authority, direct deal"

**Timeline (0-15):**
- 0: "1 year+ / just browsing"
- 5: "6-12 months"
- 10: "3-6 months"
- 15: "≤3 months / urgent"

**Engagement (0-15):**
- 0: "Reply nahi kiya / ghost"
- 5: "Ek do reply, slow response"
- 10: "Actively engaging, questions pooch raha"
- 15: "Ready for meeting, time de raha hai"

### Score Outcomes
- **80-100**: Hottest — CEO ko handoff NOW
- **60-79**: Warm — ek aur meeting chahiye
- **40-59**: Medium — nurture karo, aur info chahiye
- **0-39**: Cold — pipeline mein rakho but priority mat do

## Red Flags (Auto-DQ)

Agar yeh red flags hain to lead ko **direct "lost" ya "nurturing"** mein dalo:

1. **"Mujhe free chahiye"** — No budget, expects free work
2. **"Abhi nahi, kal poocho"** × 3 — Time waster
3. **Decision maker available nahi** — Hamesha "boss se pooch ke batata hoon"
4. **"Concept samjhao pehle"** — Too early, needs education first
5. **"Mujhe nhi pata kya chahiye"** — Undefined problem, unclear need
6. **Competitor comparison on price only** — Commodity buying mindset

## Qualification Conversation Script for SBA

### First Call / Discovery Script

**Opening (2 min):**
"Namaste [Name], thank you for your time. Main TAGS Agency ka SBA hoon. Aapki requirements ke baare mein baat karni hai jo aapne batayi thi. Pehle aap batao — aapki current situation kya hai?"

**Discovery (5-7 min):**
1. "Aapka business exactly kya karta hai?"
2. "Is time aapki sabse badi problem kya hai?"
3. "Yeh problem kab se chal rahi hai?"
4. "Aapne iske liye kya kya try kiya ab tak?"
5. "Agar yeh solve ho jaye to aapke business pe kya farak padega?"

**Qualification (5 min):**
1. "Budget ke baare mein socha hai aapne?"
2. "Decision process kaisa hai aapke yahan?"
3. "Agar sab sahi raha to kab tak start karna chahenge?"

**Close (2 min):**
"Sounds good. Main yeh sab CEO ko share karunga. Agli baat humari [propose date] pe hogi, jahan CEO aapko poora solution dikhayenge. Chalta hai?"

## Handoff Criteria for CEO

SBA ko CEO ko handoff tab karna chahiye jab yeh 3 cheezein confirm hon:

### ✅ Handoff Checklist

- [ ] Lead ne **budget** confirm kiya (range bata di)
- [ ] Lead **decision maker** hai ya uski involvement clear hai
- [ ] **Pain point** clearly identified hai
- [ ] Lead ne **meeting** ke liye haan kaha
- [ ] Lead ka **timeline** clear hai (3 mahine ya kam)
- [ ] Score ≥ **60** (CHAMP/BANT analysis)

**Handoff Brief mein yeh sab CEO ko dena:**
- Score + framework used
- Pain points (verbatim quotes)
- Budget range
- Decision process
- Suggested next step
