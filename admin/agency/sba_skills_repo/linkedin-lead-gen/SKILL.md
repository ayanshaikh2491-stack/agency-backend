# linkedin-lead-gen

LinkedIn prospecting: research profiles, send connection requests, and run
InMail / message outreach that actually gets replies.

## When to use
Triggered by: linkedin, linked in, profile, connection, inmail, sales navigator,
"find decision makers", B2B outreach.

## Workflow
1. **Identify ICP** from the CEO's brief (role: founder, marketing head, agency owner;
   industry: local biz, clinics, coaches, e-com).
2. **Research** 5-10 targets: skim their posts/About to find a hook ("saw you launched
   a clinic in Pune — congrats").
3. **Connect** with a 1-line note referencing that hook. No pitch in the invite.
4. **After they accept**, wait 1-2 days, then send value-first message:
   - "Not selling — noticed your site isn't showing in ChatGPT answers for
     '<service> in <city>'. Happy to share a 2-min audit?" 
   - That's the hook; book the call, then hand to SBA/SEO.
5. **Nurture**: comment on their posts weekly; don't spam DM.

## Guardrails
- Max 25 invites/day (LinkedIn throttles). 
- Never pitch in the connection request.
- Track accepted→replied→meeting in the workspace for CEO visibility.
