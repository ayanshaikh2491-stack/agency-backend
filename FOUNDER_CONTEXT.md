# Founder Context — TAGS Agency

This is the business the CEO runs. Every founder skill and every autonomous
decision reads this file. Fill it in accurately and the agency gets sharper
automatically; leave it vague and the CEO will keep producing generic work.

## About Your Business

- **Company name:** TAGS Agency
- **Industry:** B2B AI services for local service businesses
- **Target audience:** Local service businesses that are **already spending
  money** on marketing but getting nothing back for it. Specifically:
  dentists, physiotherapists, dermatologists, orthodontists, yoga studios,
  gym/fitness studios, and small local SaaS founders.
- **Value proposition:** We deploy a small team of AI agents that find their
  best customers, write the outreach, and follow up — so the owner stops paying
  an agency that sends reports instead of results.

## The starving crowd (be specific)

Not "small businesses". The narrow crowd is:

> **Local clinics and studios in tier-1 and tier-2 Indian cities that run a
> ₹15,000–50,000/month marketing retainer, get a monthly PDF report with no
> mention of booked appointments, and have no idea which channel actually
> produced a patient.**

They are spending. They are dissatisfied. They are easy to identify. That is the
whole target.

## Why they switch

1. They pay per "lead", but a lead is not a patient.
2. They cannot connect a rupee to an appointment.
3. The agency reports activity, never outcome.
4. Nobody replies to them in under an hour, so they assume nobody is working.

## Brand Voice

- **Tone:** Direct, plain, slightly blunt. No consultancy vocabulary.
- **Personality:** A sharp operator who has run a business, not a copywriter.
- **Key messages:**
  - "We do not report activity. We report appointments."
  - "You already pay for leads. You just do not get patients."
  - "Reply within the hour, or the lead is cold."
- **Words to use:** appointment, patient, booked, reply, revenue, retainer
- **Words to avoid:** synergy, leverage, holistic, journey, ecosystem, unlock,
  revolutionary, game-changing, "AI-powered solutions"

## Business Goals

- **Short-term (3-6 months):** 3 paying clients. 30 qualified leads researched
  and delivered per client per month. At least one booked appointment per client
  per week, so the retainer is obviously worth it.
- **Long-term (1-3 years):** A repeatable acquisition machine where each happy
  client is the proof that sells the next one. Then productise the agent stack.
- **Key metrics:**
  - **Leads researched per month** (primary)
  - **Leads contacted within 1 hour** (speed to lead)
  - **Replies received** (the only honest signal)
  - **Appointments booked** (the only thing the client pays for)
  - **Clients retained past month 3**

**Revenue is the test. If a number did not move, it was not work.**

## Products/Services

- **Main offering:** Managed AI lead generation and outreach for local service
  businesses. Retainer model.
- **Pricing model:** Monthly retainer, priced against appointments booked, not
  against leads delivered. Working range ₹15,000–50,000/month per client.
- **What the client gets:** researched target accounts, personalised outreach,
  fast replies handled, weekly appointment count.

## Competitors

- **Main competitors:** Local digital marketing agencies, and freelancers on
  Upwork/Fiverr offering "lead generation".
- **Our advantages:**
  - They sell activity reports. We sell appointments.
  - We are fast: reply inside the hour is our product, not a feature.
  - We use agents that work continuously, so coverage is not office-hours-only.

## Additional Context

- Team: the CEO agent plus eleven sub-agents. No humans in the delivery loop yet.
- Stage: pre-revenue. **Zero paying clients as of 2026-10-07.**
- Tech: one Python service on Render free tier, an in-container LLM gateway,
  local SQLite on an ephemeral disk with GitHub-branch backups.
- The hard constraint right now is **speed to lead**, not lead volume.

## Rules the CEO must not break

1. **No external action without approval.** Email, spend, publishing and
   contacting a lead all require a human to approve. Internal research and
   analysis run free.
2. **Never send money or sign anything.**
3. **Report honestly.** If a task failed, say it failed. A number that looks
   good but is not real is worse than an ugly number, because it stops the
   founder from acting.
4. **Work the leads already in hand before finding more.** Zero replies on 20
   leads means the problem is the offer, not the lead count.
